# Where Horses Run v1 release notes

Release candidate: `WHR-V1-PREPARATION-V1`  
Prepared: 2026-07-18  
Status: ready for final release decision

## What v1 provides

Where Horses Run is a static-first bilingual guide for finding horse-racing countries and regions, racecourses, racing types, reviewed meeting dates and timetable information, terminology, and official-source links.

The v1 candidate contains:

- 771 public pages;
- 387 English pages and 384 Japanese pages;
- 17 existing route families;
- 98 bilingual country and region profiles;
- 36 racecourse records rendered through 72 bilingual racecourse routes;
- 171 reviewed official-source records covering 98 countries and regions;
- reviewed Calendar, Today, Tomorrow, Current Timetable, and meeting-detail views;
- publication ranks C, B, B+, A, and A+;
- 96 bilingual glossary term routes and related-term navigation;
- complete static no-JavaScript readability;
- sitemap, canonical, hreflang, structured-data, title, description, and social metadata.

## Data and source policy

Current and complete information must be confirmed on the linked official source. Where Horses Run publishes only reviewed, publication-safe data inside its C through A+ display boundary.

The site does not claim complete global coverage or real-time updates. Coverage depth varies by country, region, racecourse, organiser, official-source structure, and review status.

## What v1 does not publish

The v1 scope excludes:

- entries and complete racecards;
- horse, jockey, trainer, draw, weight, body-weight, and other participant fields;
- odds, popularity, results, payouts, predictions, selections, and betting advice;
- copied official-source page bodies or raw HTML;
- embedded video, direct stream URLs, unofficial mirrors, or redistributed recordings;
- user accounts, submissions, personalization, public write APIs, transactions, ticketing, wagering, or payments;
- automatic translation, unreviewed generation, and unattended publication.

## Known limitations

- Meeting dates and post times may change, be postponed, or be cancelled.
- An empty current or upcoming view does not prove that no racing exists.
- A last-checked date records a review point and does not guarantee continuing validity.
- Publication rank describes the approved display depth, not completeness.
- A listed official live or replay route does not guarantee viewing access.
- Dates and times use the official source's local context and may be affected by timezone or daylight-saving changes.
- Stale, unsupported, conflicting, or incorrect information may be corrected, reduced, or removed.

## Privacy and automation

The v1 candidate adds no visitor identifiers, interaction or query logging, analytics, cookies, or client storage.

The release-readiness work does not create a release tag, publish automatically, authorize deployment, or perform deployment. Those actions remain outside this candidate review and require a separate final release decision.
