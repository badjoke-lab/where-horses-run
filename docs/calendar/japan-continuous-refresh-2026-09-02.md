# Japan continuous refresh repair — 2026-09-02

This repair keeps JRA, NAR, and Banei current/future meetings inside recurring acquisition while official programmes mature.

It addresses three failure modes:

- a meeting exists in canonical/public at C/B/B+/A and stops being retried before the official source publishes richer detail;
- a current/future meeting is absent and falls outside recurring coverage-gap discovery;
- a one-time acquisition is retired and accidentally becomes the effective current-window acquisition path.

The due-job policy therefore requires all three Japan systems to keep regular refresh, coverage-gap discovery, source revalidation, and rank retry enabled across at least the current 30-day horizon.

This does not make A+ mandatory and does not cap publication at A. Acquisition/classification continues to use the five-rank contract and may remain at C/B/B+/A when the official evidence does not support a higher rank.

This repair does not enable automatic approval, promotion, publication, merge, or deployment. Acquired improvements remain review-only until the existing review/promotion boundary is satisfied.
